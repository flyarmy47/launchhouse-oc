How it works

01

Book

Book your time slot online through BayOS using your mobile number.

02

Reserve

Reserve your date and time for up to 4 players, in 30-minute blocks. Only 4 players can be in the bay at one time, and you need to book at least 30 minutes before you start.

03

Get your code

Thirty minutes before your session you'll get a text with your door code. It works from 10 minutes before your start time.

04

Play

Arrive no more than 10 minutes early, when your code starts working. Let yourself in, play, and clean up when you're done, just like an Airbnb.

Contact Us

Cancellation policy

This is a strict policy, nothing personal. We hold the bay for you, and a last-minute cancellation means we can't rebook it.

How to cancel

Cancel your booking in BayOS at irvine.bayos.golf. Having trouble? Call or text (714) 477-7231 before your cancellation deadline.

12+ hours before

Cancel for a full refund to your original payment method. Members get their included hours or credits back.

Under 12 hours

You forfeit the full reservation amount (hours booked × rate). Members forfeit any Launch House Pass hours or loyalty credits used.

Need to reschedule?

Inside the 12-hour window, you can move your booking once to any open slot within the next 7 days. Contact us before your start time.

No-shows

If you don't show up, you forfeit the full reservation amount. No exceptions.

Running late?

You still finish at your original end time. If the slot after yours is open, book an extension in BayOS.

If we cancel

If we close the bay for maintenance, an equipment outage, or anything else on our end, you get a full refund or credit, your choice.

Membership & loyalty

Launch House Pass is $100/month and includes 4 hours of sim credits, then $25/hour after that, plus booking up to 14 days out. Every booking earns 20% back in credits toward future bookings: spend $300 and you'll earn 60 credits, a full hour at the public rate. Members earn credits on paid hours beyond their 4 included hours.
